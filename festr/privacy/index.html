Privacy

Your plans stay on your iPhone.

Festr has no account and collects no personal data. Your favourite festivals and chosen sets are stored only on your device.

The app downloads festival schedules from a public feed. Those requests contain no information about you beyond what any web request includes (such as your IP address), and they are not used to identify or track you.

When you save a wallpaper, Festr asks for add-only access to Photos. It can add images but never read your library.

There are no analytics, ads or third-party SDKs. Questions: support@worldwidewebb.co.
