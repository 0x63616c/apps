Are the set times accurate?

Festr shows the latest times it has, and each festival shows when they were last updated. Times can change on the day, so check the festival's own channels too.

How do I set a wallpaper?

In the Wallpaper tab, choose a day and theme and tap Save to Photos. Then open the image in Photos, tap Share › Use as Wallpaper.

Why did my Live Activity end?

iOS ends Live Activities after about eight hours. Festr starts the activity once the rest of your day fits, and ends it after your last set.
