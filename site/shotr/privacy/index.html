Privacy · Shotr

Shotr processes your photo library on your iPhone. Screenshot text and tags are stored locally. Shotr has no account, analytics, or remote AI service.

Photo access is controlled in iOS Settings. Shotr only reads the photos you allow. Local data may be included in device backups according to your iOS settings.

If you email support, your email and any information you choose to send are used to respond.
