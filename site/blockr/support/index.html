How can we help?

Screen Time access

Open Blockr and allow Screen Time access. If access was revoked, pick your apps again on this device.

A lock did not arrive

Each device needs its own setup and a connection. Check iCloud status in Blockr Settings. iCloud is unavailable in builds without a configured container.

Mac protection

Native Mac app blocking is unavailable. Timed websites require an approved, signed content filter; unavailable builds report this in Settings.
