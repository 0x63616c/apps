What Blockr stores

Blockr stores groups, local app picks, lock history and shield-button taps in its shared on-device container. App picks and opaque Screen Time tokens never sync. When iCloud is enabled in a signed build, group names, typed websites, category hints, durations and lock schedules sync through your private CloudKit database. No Blockr account is required. This build shows iCloud unavailable until its container is configured.

No tracking

No analytics, advertising or crash-reporting SDKs, and no tracking across apps or websites.

Deleting your data

Reset this device in Settings removes the local replica and picks when no lock is active. It does not delete iCloud records; synced groups can return. Manage iCloud storage in system settings to remove cloud data. Removing the app may leave shared-container data while its extensions remain installed.
