Festr is a simple, fast way to plan a music festival weekend on your iPhone.

DISCOVER
• Upcoming festivals across the US with dates, city and a countdown
• Tap the heart to keep the ones you're going to in Favourites

SET TIMES
• Each day's lineup as a clean timeline: stage, time and artist
• Times are always shown in the festival's local time zone, including sets that run past midnight
• Tap a set to add it to My Schedule; clashes are highlighted straight away

MY SCHEDULE
• Your chosen sets, day by day
• See at a glance where two picks overlap

LOCK-SCREEN WALLPAPER
• Turn your day, or the full lineup, into a wallpaper at your iPhone's full resolution
• The top is left clear for the clock
• Choose from three themes and save one day or every day to Photos

LIVE ACTIVITY
• Follow the day from your Lock Screen and Dynamic Island: who's on now, a progress bar, and what's next
• Starts on festival days, or start and stop it yourself

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account and no tracking. Your favourites and schedule stay on your iPhone.

Set times change. Festr shows the latest times it has and when they were last updated; check the festival's own channels on the day. Festr is an independent app and is not affiliated with any festival or promoter.
